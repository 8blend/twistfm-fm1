# TWISTER

TWISTER is an unofficial firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, inspired by the OPL3 sound of the Twisted Electrons TWISTfm:
eight voices, a 4-operator layer and a 2-operator layer with the eight OPL3 waveforms, a 4-pole filter per voice
with 15 modes (LIQUID and MS20 flavours, HEAT), and a chain of four effects: distortion, phaser, tape echo
(free or tempo-synced) and reverb (room, plate, long shimmer).

It also has three LFOs with modulation chains, a modulation envelope, vibrato, tremolo and portamento; an
arpeggiator, a 128-step sequencer, scales and chords; 16 user presets saved on the FM-1; MIDI in from USB and
the TRS jack, MIDI out and clock out on USB, and sync to an external MIDI clock. It comes with two preset packs:
drum'n'bass (reese, neuro, tech bass, stabs) and dub / psychedelic techno (dub chords, acid, rolling bass).

This is version {{VERSION}}, a development build. You install it at your own risk; the installer can put
M-VAVE's own firmware back.

- [Install](install/) it from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- [Download {{PKG}}](firmware/{{PKG}}).
